Welcome

There's a kind of light in the foothills. Soft, golden, unhurried. It's the same feeling I want every gallery to carry.

Pine & Brook Photography is based in El Dorado Hills and works throughout Sacramento, Folsom, Cameron Park, Placerville, and the surrounding Sierra foothills wine country. I photograph engagements, couples, families, and portraits with a documentary eye and a soft, natural hand in editing.
