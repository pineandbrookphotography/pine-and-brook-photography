About Pine & Brook

Hi, I'm Veronica Zapata, the eye behind the camera.

I'm a mom of five, which means I understand chaos better than almost anyone. Mismatched shoes, someone always mid-giggle or mid-meltdown, a house that's rarely quiet. But more than that, being knee-deep in that beautiful chaos every single day has taught me just how meaningful these fleeting moments really are. That's exactly what I show up to capture for every family, couple, and individual who lets me into their world for an hour or two.

I started Pine & Brook Photography because I wanted to spend my days doing exactly this: sitting with people in their most unguarded moments and handing them back something they'll keep forever. I'm based in El Dorado Hills and photograph all across the greater Sacramento area and Sierra foothills, from vineyards and oak-lined trails to quiet backyards and everywhere in between.

I've been doing this for about ten years now, and my approach really hasn't changed much: keep it real, use the light that's already there, and don't overdo the editing. I'm not trying to make you look like someone else. Whether it's an engagement session, a solo portrait, or a family gathering, I'm mostly there to catch the little things you'll actually want to remember later.
